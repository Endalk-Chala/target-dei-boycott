# Public-source collection and coding protocol (working draft)

## Question
How do publics, organizers, and a corporation construct or contest the obligations of a brand following the 2025 withdrawal of publicly communicated DEI commitments?

## Periods
- **A / initial response:** 2025-01-24 to 2025-04-30 inclusive.
- **Middle / continuity (exploratory):** 2025-05-01 to 2026-01-31.
- **B / later public response:** 2026-02-01 to 2026-03-31 inclusive.

Retain period-level search logs and attrition. The middle period is not automatically pooled into the two-period design.

## Search
The prepared collector searches terms including `Target boycott`, `Target DEI`, `#BoycottTarget`, `Target Fast`, `"Target" "DEI"`, and `"Target" "belonging"`. Its source rows can be re-fetched using known DIDs and rkeys. Preserve query, API endpoint, date range, sorting, run timestamp and any errors, including authorization or pagination failures. **These API queries have not run successfully in this environment.**

## Inclusion
Include posts that explicitly discuss Target's **2025 DEI rollback**, the associated boycott, corporate responses, or contemporaneous arguments for/against it. Retain opposing and neutral views. Posts with a bare hashtag, generic retailer criticism, quoted links, or multiple retailer references require manual screening. Separate a corporate advertisement from audience comments about it. Do not include the 2023 Target Pride campaign or unrelated 2026 ICE boycott without separately defined bridging analysis.

## Audit and analyses
1. Freeze search strategy and eligibility rules before coding.
2. Archive source URLs and metadata; keep full text private pending ethical review.
3. Deduplicate by AT URI (DID + rkey), keeping all retrieval paths.
4. Conduct a blind pilot on a stratified subsample with at least two coders when feasible.
5. Record decisions and intercoder reliability by variable; never report calculated results until coding completes.
6. Maintain attrition and missing-content tables and disclose limits to historical search coverage and platform indexing.

## Public release policy
Publish source links, timestamps, limited excerpts, search logs and documented protocols. Avoid republishing private details, embedded media, and politically sensitive quotations at scale. Do not publicly infer ordinary users' demographics or political affiliation. Reassess ethics review requirements before the full study.
