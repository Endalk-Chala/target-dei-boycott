# Brand Covenant Broken — Expanded TikTok discovery / contextual audit (Batch 03)

This is a versioned **discovery snapshot**, not a coded TikTok dataset or a platform-representative sample.

- **28 unique exact TikTok video-ID leads** (24 carried from Batch02, 4 newly found).
- ID-derived time bands: **19** initial Jan–Apr 2025; **8** middle May 2025–Jan 2026; **1** late Feb–Mar 2026.
- **Zero direct video playback/content verification**, **zero confirmed posting dates**, **zero coded observations**.
- Includes **12 unresolved creator/comment research leads** (comments are *not* videos), **27 contextual events/document records**, and an exclusion log.
- The first 24 videos are in the [Batch 02 collection](../batch02_2026-11-04/); this directory contains the consolidated 28-lead table and expanded context.
- **Original inferential windows remain unchanged** (Jan 24–Apr 30 2025 and Feb 1–Mar 31 2026). May 2025–Jan 2026 is exploratory to understand movement continuity; do not quietly pool it into the original inferential sample.
- Several new leads have **weak or no DEI-topic evidence**; they are retained *for screening only*, not listed as eligible videos. Do not count documentary context, news quotes, TikTok comments, or creators without exact IDs as video observations.
- TikTok ID-derived timestamps and third-party mirrors are hints, **not** verification.
- Discovery relies on news and search indexing, which can strongly overselect viral English-language posts. Do not infer prevalence or representativeness from this table.

Research stewardship: use platform access and documented human review for inclusion; minimize redistribution of creators' identifiable material.
