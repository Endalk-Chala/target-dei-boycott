- Direct TikTok video page access was restricted. Public news excerpts and third-party snippets **must not** be substituted for audiovisual observation.
- The 60 tasks in search_matrix.csv are **not yet executed on TikTok**; do not call them completed systematic sampling.

## Collection method

Each of 10 temporal strata has six prewritten search strings. Execute them in TikTok's public Videos search, noting actual visible search controls and sorting. Capture up to the **first 20 distinct video IDs per query** in displayed order (or the observed count if fewer), before selectively filtering by topic. Record rank, search timestamp, exact URL, posted date, source content verified, eligibility and reason for exclusion. Deduplicate by numeric video ID while retaining multiple discovery paths. Do not claim this is random sampling of all TikTok posts.

Original study windows remain: A = **2025-01-24 through 2025-04-30**, B = **2026-02-01 through 2026-03-31**. The intervening **2025-05-01 through 2026-01-31** is a separate exploratory continuity frame. Separate the 2025 DEI protest from the 2023 Pride and 2026 ICE controversies, unless a creator explicitly links them.
