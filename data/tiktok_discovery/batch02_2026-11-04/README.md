## Data stewardship

This is a **discovery leads inventory, not publishable human-subjects-ready evidence**. It contains public social-media account identifiers and link references to politically sensitive expression; minimize re-identification and quote redistribution. Preserve distinction between source articles and primary TikTok video observations, and do not infer content or engagement beyond available evidence.

The eight sheets were exported as CSV to support GitHub diffs and reproducibility; formatting, formulas and workbook metadata are not included in the CSV exports. The original Excel workbook is retained separately in the research working files.
