# Brand Covenant Broken
### Digital activism, corporate communication, and community accountability in the Target DEI boycott

**Researcher:** Endalkachew H. Chala, PhD  
**Research status (2026-11-04):** **Working paper in preparation** — not submitted or peer reviewed  
**Research field:** Public relations, corporate social advocacy, brand relationships, digital activism and platform communication

## Project summary
This study investigates how Target's January 2025 changes to its DEI commitments became the subject of public boycott organizing and contested expectations of corporate responsibility. It juxtaposes the company's publicly communicated commitments and subsequent messaging with original public social-media expressions and organizer communications.

**Central question:** What happens to a corporation's relational obligations when it retreats from prior social commitments that some publics interpret as enduring brand promises?

This is a working theoretical and methodological framing. There are **no reported empirical findings** at this stage.
